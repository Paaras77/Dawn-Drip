DUSK & DRIP: PROMPTS

================================================================================
STEP 1: SET UP THE PAGE
================================================================================
Make a/Dusk & Drip coffee shop website. Start with index.html in the public folder. The 
tagline is 'Brewed from dusk 'til dawn.' Use clean, accessible HTML, and link Playfair 
Display and DM Sans from Google Fonts, plus style.css and script.js

================================================================================
STEP 2: BUILD ALL THE SECTIONS
================================================================================
Put a sticky navbar at the top with the shop name on the left. On the right, add links to 
Home, Menu and Contact, a hamburger button for mobile and a cart button with a little count 
badge.

- Hero: full height, with a space for the sky and moon, the headline 'Brewed from dusk 
  'til dawn.', the line 'Your haven for midnight roasts and sunrise brews.' and 
   a View Menu button.
- Menu: a title, filter chips (All, Hot Drinks, Cold Drinks, Snacks), a search box and 12 
  cards. Each card has a photo, a name, a one-line description, a price and an Add to Order 
  button.
- Contact: 404 Twilight Way, Nightfall Quarter, open 6 PM to 6 AM, a tappable phone number 
  and email, and a spot for an open/closed sign.
- Footer: the shop name, the tagline and a copyright with the current year.

The 12 items:
- Hot Drinks: Dusk Espresso $3.25, Owl's Latte $4.75, Copper Cortado $4.25, Velvet Mocha $5.00
- Cold Drinks: Slow Drip Cold Brew $4.95, Nitro Nightfall $5.50, Iced Amber Latte $5.25, 
- Salted Caramel Frappe $5.95
- Snacks: Almond Dawn Croissant $3.95, Dark Side Brownie $3.50, Cinnamon Dusk Roll $4.25, 
- Avocado Sunrise Toast $7.50

================================================================================
STEP 3: ADD THE POP-UPS
================================================================================
Add a cart that slides in from the right, with the items, a subtotal, a Place order 
button and a close button. Add a small toast message that pops up when something is added, 
and a back-to-top button.

================================================================================
STEP 4: STYLE IT
================================================================================
Make it look like a cozy coffee bar after dark.

- Colours: dark espresso browns, warm cream text, copper buttons and amber highlights.
- Fonts and shape: Playfair Display for headings, DM Sans for body text, with rounded 
  corners.
- Navbar: a blurred, see-through navbar stuck to the top.
- Buttons and cards: pill buttons that lift when you hover. Menu cards with a photo that 
  zooms slightly and a soft amber glow on hover.
- Mobile menu: under 900px, collapse the links into a hamburger menu.
- Layout: 1 menu column on phones, 2 on tablets, 3 on laptops and 4 on big screens, with no 
  sideways scrolling at any size.

================================================================================
STEP 5: MAKE THE MENU WORK
================================================================================
Make the filter buttons and the search box work together, with a gentle fade when cards 
change. If nothing matches, show 'Nothing by that name. Try 'latte' or 'brownie'?' and a 
Clear search button that resets everything.

================================================================================
STEP 6: MAKE THE CART WORK
================================================================================
Add to Order should add the item, or add one more if it's already there, then update the 
badge and show a toast saying 'Added [item] to your order.' In the cart, show each item with 
minus, plus and remove buttons, and a live total. Save the cart so it survives a refresh, 
even if storage is blocked. Place order should clear the cart and say 'Order in. 
Your cup is dripping its way to you.', then close after a couple of seconds. Opening and 
closing should work with the cart button, the close button, clicking outside and the Escape 
key.

================================================================================
STEP 7: MOBILE MENU AND SCROLLING
================================================================================
The hamburger should open and close the mobile menu, stop the page scrolling behind it and 
close when you tap a link. Highlight the current section in the navbar as you scroll.

================================================================================
STEP 8: BRING IN THE DUSK-TO-DAWN MAGIC
================================================================================
Give the hero twinkling stars and a glowing crescent moon that drifts a little with the 
mouse.

Change the sky colours by time of day: dawn is copper and rose, daytime is muted amber, 
dusk is amber and violet, and night is deep navy-espresso.

Change the greeting too:
- Night: 'Still up? The kettle is on.'
- Morning: 'Dawn already? Welcome, early bird.'
- Afternoon: 'Afternoon pick-me-up?'
- Dusk: 'Golden hour is coffee hour.'

Make the open sign use a moon: full moon when open, crescent when closing soon, new moon 
when closed.

When someone adds an item, drip an amber droplet toward the cart and pulse the badge once. 
Fade sections in as you scroll, and add the amber glow on hover.

If a visitor has reduced motion turned on, switch off the animations and parallax.

================================================================================
STEP 9: FINAL POLISH
================================================================================
Check the whole site

- Focus: keyboard focus should always be visible, in amber.
- Labels: icon-only buttons need labels.
- Cart focus: the cart should trap focus while open and give it back when closed.
- Colours: text should have enough contrast.
- Back to top: show the button only after scrolling 600px.
- Footer year: set it automatically.
- Page title and description: add a title and description for the page.
- Mobile: check 360, 768, 1024 and 1440px, with no sideways scroll, touch targets of at least 
  44px and no text under 16px.

================================================================================
STEP 10:LAST MISSING FINAL PAYMENT
================================================================================
When I add items to the cart and click "Place order", nothing real happens. It just clears 
the cart. Please add a proper checkout pop-up on the page itself.

When someone clicks "Place order", close the cart drawer and open a checkout pop-up in the 
middle of the screen. It should have three parts:

1. Order summary: list the items they picked, with the name, quantity and price for each one. 
Underneath, show the subtotal, the total. Add a small "Edit order" link that goes 
back to the cart.

2. Your details: ask for full name, email and phone number. Add a simple choice between 
"Pickup" and "Dine in".

3. Payment method: let them pick one of these with radio buttons: "Card", "Pay at the counter"
 and "Digital wallet". If they pick Card, show card number, name on card, expiry date and CVV. 
 Space the card number out as they type, and format the expiry as MM/YY. For the other two options, 
 hide the card fields and show a short friendly note instead.
